Quick FAQ
How accurate is the diagnosis?
The AI model achieves ~92% accuracy. For critical decisions, consult a plant pathologist.
What image formats work?
JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WEBP formats are supported.
Is my data stored?
Diagnosis history is stored locally in your browser only.

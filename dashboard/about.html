About the System

Plant Disease Detector is an AI-powered web application that helps farmers, gardeners, and plant enthusiasts identify plant diseases quickly and accurately using just a photo of the affected leaf.

Powered by the EfficientNetB4 deep learning model trained on over 61,000 images across 38 disease classes and 14 plant species, the system provides instant diagnosis with confidence scores and detailed treatment recommendations.
